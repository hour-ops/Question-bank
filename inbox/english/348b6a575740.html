__________________________________________________________________

__________________________________________________________________

3. He will be back in two days.

__________________________________________________________________

4. They have a meeting once a week.

__________________________________________________________________

5. The little boy was late for class again.

__________________________________________________________________

6. My mobile phone was lost on the way to school.

__________________________________________________________________

7. They decided not to go there again.

__________________________________________________________________

8. It’s very wet and cold today.

__________________________________________________________________

9. My bike is broken.

__________________________________________________________________

__________________________________________________________________

 

V翻译填空。

1. 他的计划最后证明是成功的。

His plan ______________________ successful in the end.(turn)

2.这个周日将会有一场足球赛。

There ______________________ a football match this Sunday.(be)

1. 我们已把我们所有的朋友都邀请来了。

We ______________________ all our friends to come here.(invite)

2. 我听见有人正在教室弹钢琴。

I hear someone ______________________ in the classroom.(piano)

3. Tom刚刚让我们保持安静。

Tom made us ______________________ just now.(keep)

参考答案：

I

1. 主谓

2. 主谓宾

3. 主系表

4. 主谓间宾直宾

5. 主谓宾宾补

6. 主系表

7. 主谓宾宾补

8. 主谓宾

9. 主系表

10. 主谓直宾间宾

II

1. He didn’t come late for class yesterday.

2. Ms. White didn’t lend me any books.

3. You had better not take your mother’s advice.

4. They are not going to have a trip this Sunday.

5. We needn’t return books by ten.

III

1. Does Mr. Brown have to take exercise every morning?

2. Did Mr. Zhang teach her math last year?

3. Are there any birds in that tall tree?

4. Has Betty done very well in her English exam?

5. Can Tom speak French fluently.

IV

1. How did he go to work?

2. How long did he spend cleaning the room?

3. How soon will he be back?

4. How often do they have a meeting?

5. Who was late for class again?

6. Whose mobile phone was lost on the way to school?

7. What did they decide not to do?

8. What’s the weather like today?

9. What’s wrong with your bike?

10. Why did he go to the hospital?

  Ⅴ

1. turned out   2. will be/is going to be   3. have invited   4.playing the piano   5.keep quiet/silent
