26年湖北专升本英语阅读真题及答案

来源：湖北专升本网 浏览次数：1333 发布时间：2026-04-17 14:30:17

4月17日湖北专升本英语考试结束后，考生分享了真题卷阅读题型，普本课堂老师第一时间提供了正确答案，一起来看看吧。

Passage 1

When a cat named Tiantian went missing in Beijing last year.....

The profession first appeared in Western countries about twenty years ago but it came into being in China only recently.

The job requires more than just skills. One detective told the reporter that his longest search lasted six daysHe walked through the city for hours each day until he finally found the missing dog behind a factory. This showsthat to be a pet detective, one also needs patience.

For pet owners, these detectives give them emotional support. When a pet goes missing, the owners often feelhelpless and worried. Having someone who knows what to do gives them hope.

The Pet Detective

When its first appearance in 17.________countries about twenty years ago, the profession came into being in China only recently.

Duty: The job is to find lost pets using skills like tracking, 18.________, and even using search dogs

A Real Case: One search lasted six days, showing that pet detective should have 19.________.

From pet detectives, the owners can get emotional 20.________

Pets are now regarded as 21.________.


17.【答案】Westerr

【考点】事实细节题[解]"The profession first appeared in Western countries about twenty years ago but it came intobeingin Chinaonly recently.”意为:这个职业最早出现在西方国家大约是20年前，而在中国是最近才出现的。对比原文，发现 countries 前缺少定语 Westemn.注意，介词短语in Westem countries 在原文中做动词appeared的地点状语，而在题目中做名词appearance的后置定语。

18. 【答案】 searching

【考点]事实细节题

解析】该题目缺少原文。但结合题干发现是在探讨宠物侦探的职责，他们需要追踪、搜寻宠物，甚至需要借助搜救犬。答案searching在题干中与tracking, using是并列成分，根据语法平行原则，这一空也需要填写动名词 searching。

19.【答案】 patience

【考点】细节归纳题

【解析】第三段讲述了一名宠物侦探的经历，他最长的搜查持续了6天。他每天在城里走几个小时，直到最后在一家工厂后面找到了那只失踪的狗。第三段最后一句“This shows that to be a pet detective, one also needs patience.”意为:这表明，要成为一名宠物侦探还需要耐心。是对上文的概括和总结，因此题干中缺少宾语 patience.

20. 【答案】 support

【考点】事实细节题

【解析】第四段第一句“For pet owners, these detectives give them emotional support."意为:于宠物主人来说,这些侦探给了他们情感上的支持。题干意为:从宠物侦探那里，主人可以得到情感上的.....。因此需要填写的是宾语support。注意，此处涉及到动词的同义替换，将give sb.sth.，给某人某物，转换为sb.get sth.,某人得到某物。

21. 【答案】 family

【考点】事实细节题

【解析】该题目缺少原文。不过结合题干:宠物被视为....·，可推测出，原文应该是表达宠物已经被看作家人，故答案是family。该题涉及动词短语be regarded as..，被视为。


Passage 2

For many years, scientists thought bad childhood experiences could harm the brain for life. But a new study from Germany shows that exercise can help the brain recover.

The researchers studied 75 adults who had difficult childhoods. Some had been hurt or well cared for when they were young. The researchers used brain scans(脑部扫描) to see how different parts of the brain worker together. They found that in people who exercised often, brain areas for feelings and stress were closely connected. Exercise, they said, helped the brain work better.

The team also found that there was a best amount of exercise: about 150 to 390 minutes a week. Doing more exercise did not always bring better results.

Why can exercise help? This is because exercise can build new links between brain cells and lower stress in the body. For people with difficult childhood, exercise is like a natural medicine that helps the brain recover.

One researcher said, “A difficult childhood can make life harder, but it does not decide a person’s future.” We cannot change the past, but we can do something now to build a better future.

Exercise Heals the Brain

Misconception: Bad ____22.____ experiences could harm the brain for life.

Participants: 75 ____23.____who had difficult childhoods.

Findings: In people who exercised often, brain areas for feelings and ____24.____ were closely connected. 

A best ____25.____ of exercise: about 150 to 390 minutes a week.

Conclusion: A difficult childhood does not decide a person’s ____26.____, because exercise can heal the brain.


参考答案解析：

22.【答案】childhood

【考点】事实细节题

【解析】第一段第一句“For many years, scientists thought bad childhood experiences could harm the brain for life.”意为：多年来，科学家们认为糟糕的童年经历会对大脑造成终身伤害。对比题干，缺少定语childhood。

23.【答案】adults

【考点】事实细节题

【解析】第二段第一句“The researchers studied 75 adults who had difficult childhoods.”意为：研究人员研究了75名童年经历坎坷的成年人。对比题干，缺少定语从句的先行词adults。

24.【答案】stress

【考点】事实细节题

【解析】第二段倒数第二句“They found that in people who exercised often, brain areas for feelings and stress were closely connected.”意为：他们发现，在经常锻炼的人群中，情感和压力的大脑区域是紧密相连的。对比题干，缺少介词for后与feelings并列的宾语stress。

25.【答案】amount

【考点】事实细节题

【解析】第三段第一句“The team also found that there was a best amount of exercise”意为：研究团队还发现，运动量也有一个最佳值。对比题干，缺少宾语从句的主语amount。

26.【答案】future

【考点】事实细节题

【解析】最后一段第一句One researcher said, “A difficult childhood can make life harder, but it does not decide a person’s future.”意为：一位研究人员说：“艰难的童年会让生活变得更加艰难，但它并不能决定一个人的未来。”对比题干，缺少decide的宾语，同时也是a person’s future这个名词短语的中心词future。


Passage 3

On the first day of school, our professor challenged us to get to know someone we didn't already know. I stood up to look around when a gentle hand touched my shoulder. I turned round to find a little old lady looking up at me with a smile. She said, "Hi, handsome! I'm Rose. I'm 87 years old. Can I give you a hug?" I laughed, "Of course! Why are you in college at such a young, innocent age?" I asked. She jokingly replied, "I'm here to meet a rich husband, get married, have children and then travel around." "No seriously," I asked. 

I was curious why she took on this challenge at this age. "I always dreamed of having a college education, and now I'm getting one," she told me. We became friends. Every day for the next three months we would leave class together and talk for hours. Listening to this human "time machine" as she shared her years of wisdom and experience with me was so interesting. Over the year, Rose became a campus icon and she easily made friends wherever she went. 

She loved to dress up and enjoyed the attention from other students. She was living it up. At the end of the term, we invited Rose to speak at a student gathering. I'll never forget what she taught us. "We do not stop playing because we are old; we grow old because we stop playing. There are only a few secrets to staying young. You have to laugh and find humor every day. 

You've got to have a dream. When you lose your dreams, you die. There are many people walking around who are dead, and they don't even know it," she said. "There is a huge difference between growing older and growing up. Anybody can grow older. That doesn't take any talent or ability," she added. "But growing up requires finding the opportunity in change. Have no regrets. People usually don't have regrets for what they did, but rather for things they did not do." Rose taught by example that it's never too late to make your dream come true.

31.罗斯用自己的经历告诉我们：实现梦想，永远都不晚。


Passage 4

It was getting dark. The old man sat by the window of his small house, watching the River Esk flow by gently. Memories flooded his mind. He remembered the days when he was young and strong, when he would fish in the river and catch enough to feed his family.

Time went by fast. His children had grown up and moved out, and his wife had passed away. The old man continued to fish, but now fishing only meant a connection with the past.

After sunset, the old man picked up his fishing rod and walked down to the river bank. He cast his line into the water, waiting for a bite. Suddenly, his line moved, and he pulled in the line with great excitement. It was a big fish! In the past, he would have been proud of catching such a fish. But now, it was just a reminder of the passing of time. He let out a deep breath and decided to set the fish free.

When the old man walked back to his home, he felt lighter and freer. He sat by the window, watching the river flow by, and smiled. He knew that he would always be by the river, and that it would always be with him.
