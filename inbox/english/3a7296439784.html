湖北普通专升本英语模拟题及答案（四）

来源：湖北专升本网 浏览次数：1009 发布时间：2025-09-30 10:56:27

对于备考26届湖北专升本的同学来说，英语科目是非常重要的。同学们可以通过多做模拟题，检验自己是否掌握了解题技巧，做题也是顺利通过考试的基石。

VI. 翻译填空。

1. 你的房间都是灰尘，需要打扫。

 Your room is full of dust. It ______________________. (clean)

2. 当你驾驶的时候，你越小心越好。

You ______________________ when you are driving. (careful)

3. 她还在镜子前，没有动。

She is still ______________________. She hasn’t moved yet. (mirror)

    4. 我今晚绝对不能晚睡，因为我明天不得不早起。

I mustn’t go to bed late tonight because I ______________________ tomorrow. (get up)

    5. 这幅画使我想起了我父亲。

This picture ______________________. (remind)

 

Ⅶ. 短文填空，每空一词。

It was early and there weren't many people on the road. A girl on a bike passed me 1.___________(quick). She was riding on ice when suddenly she 2.___________ (fall) off her bike a few steps in front 3.___________ me. She was trying hard to get up but fell again. Without thinking, I went over and helped her stand up. She said "Thank you!" to me 4.___________ a smile and then rode away. The words "Thank you" 5.___________ (remind) me of my friends in my old school, which made me feel 6.___________ (little) nervous about my new school.

When I finally got to school, I was led into the classroom and 7.___________ (ask) to sit beside a girl. I was so nervous. I knew some of the students were looking at me 8.___________ I didn't dare to look at 9.___________ (they). Soon the first class started. It was English. They used a different textbook from my old school and I didn't have a copy. As I was wondering what to do, a book appeared. "Let's share!" the girl next to me said. I looked up. It was the girl I helped that morning. What a surprise! Thanks to her, I 10.___________ (need) be worried.


参考答案：

I

1. “你说话没必要这样不客气，”我的妻子边回答边照着镜子。

2. 我坐在一个新式的满是网眼儿的椅子上，等待着。

3. “我们不应该买我们不需要的东西，”我突然发表意见说。

4. 我也不必提醒你昨天买的那条糟糕透了的领带。

5. 女人有多少帽子也不嫌多。

II

1. mustn’t              2. needn’t           3. mustn’t              4. needn’t              5. needn’t

6. mustn’t              7. needn’t              8. mustn’t              9. needn’t              10. needn’t

III

1. need;need      2. Do you need to     3. about      4. does, look like    5. in front of

VI

1. needs cleaning/needs to be cleaned

2. can’t/ can never be too careful

3. in front of the mirror

4. have to get up early

5. reminds/reminded me of my father

Ⅶ

1. quickly   2. fell   3. of  4. with    5.reminded  

6. less   7. asked   8. but   9. them   10. needn’t
