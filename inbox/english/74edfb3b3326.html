26届湖北专升本英语高频语法练习题及答案（一）

来源：湖北专升本网 浏览次数：727 发布时间：2025-11-02 15:23:16

对于备考26届湖北专升本的同学来说，英语科目是非常重要的。同学们可以通过多做模拟题，检验自己是否掌握了解题技巧，做题也是顺利通过考试的基石。

Ⅱ 用所给词的适当形式填空。
1. I had no desire________(see) the man make fool of himself.

2. My English teacher warned me________(not,speak) Chinese in the classroom.

3. I've heard him________(speak) about you often.

4. My father is always the last________(arrive) home everyday.

5. He hurried to the hospital, only________(tell) his father had just died.

 

Ⅲ 找出下划线的错误之处并改正。

1. If the building project to complete by the end of this month is delayed, the construction company will be fined.

_________________________________

2. Though he had often made his little sister to cry，today he was made to cry by his little sister.

_________________________________

3. The teacher told them to not make so much noise.

_________________________________

4. She did nothing but to look at the piano.

_________________________________

5. It was cruel for him not to give his son a hand when in need.

_________________________________

Ⅳ 翻译填空。

1. 当我的父亲年轻的时候，像许多工人一样，他被要求从早到晚工作。

When my father was young, he was asked ______________________ from morning till night like many other workers. (work)

2. 他继续说道：尊重他人和被他人尊重一样重要。

He went on to say that it was as important to respect others as ______________________ by others. (respect)

3. 因为飞机起飞的时间延迟了，所以现在我们无所事事除了等待。

The departure time of the plane has been postponed, so we have nothing to do now but ______________________. (wait)

4. 这个新的发明是为了让日常生活更便捷，而不是为了让它更困难。

The new invention is to make our daily life easier, ______________________ it harder. (make)

5. 据报道，他的许多小说去年已经被翻译成多门外语。

Many of his novels are reported ______________________ into several foreign languages last year. (translate)


参考答案：

Ⅱ. 用所给词的适当形式填空

1. to see   2. not to speak   3. speak   4. to arrive   5. to be told

Ⅲ. 找出下划线的错误之处并改正。

 1. to complete 改为 to be completed 2. to cry 改为 cry 3. to not make 改为not to make 4. to look at 改为 look at 5. for 改为of

Ⅳ. 翻译填空

1. to work   2. to be respected   3. wait   4. not to make    5. to have been translated
